## 1. User journeys

1. As a calling system, I want to send transactions as JSON and have them treated exactly as if they had been read
   off a statement — same mapping, same duplicate protection, same qbXML.
2. As the owner, I want my arithmetic checked, not corrected: if the control total disagrees with the rows, nothing
   posts and no amount is quietly adjusted.
3. As a caller, I do not want one oversized or misdated row to fail a batch of hundreds.
4. As the owner, I want a duplicate caught even when the same transaction arrives once from a statement and once from
   the API.

## 4. Decisions

- **Refuse the batch vs hold the row.** A control total that does not add up means the caller and this app disagree
  about money — there is no safe partial interpretation, so nothing is read out of the request at all. A single row
  that is too large, too old or missing an account is a *row* problem, so it is held with a reason and the rest of the
  batch proceeds (FR-A-8: one bad line must not fail hundreds).
- **`SourceKind` and `Direction` are derived, never supplied.** If a caller could state the direction, two callers
  could describe one movement differently, produce different fingerprints, and G4 would miss the duplicate.
- **The control total covers held rows too.** It describes what the caller submitted; excluding held rows would let a
  correct total look wrong, or hide a real disagreement.
- **Nothing is read out of a refused request.** Returning partial lines alongside errors invites a caller — or a
  future endpoint — to post them anyway.
